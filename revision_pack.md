FULL SPEC COVERAGE MAP (A–F)
A: IT Systems & Devices
Must know:
•	Types of devices (PC, mobile, servers, IoT) 
•	Input / output / storage 
•	Operating systems + functions 
•	User interfaces 
•	Choosing systems (cost, performance, security) 
 Exam focus:
•	“Explain function of…” (3–4 marks) 
•	“Give examples…” (2 marks) 
________________________________________
B: Data Transmission
Must know:
•	Wired vs wireless (Wi-Fi, Ethernet, Bluetooth) 
•	Networks (LAN, WAN, VPN) 
•	Protocols (HTTP, HTTPS, SMTP, IMAP) 
•	Bandwidth + latency 
•	Compression (lossy vs lossless) 
Exam focus:
•	“Explain how data is transmitted” (4–6 marks) 
•	“Compare connection types” 
________________________________________
C: Operating Online
Must know:
•	Cloud computing (IaaS, PaaS, SaaS) 
•	Remote working (VPNs) 
•	Online systems + communities 
Exam focus:
•	9 marker on cloud suitability 
•	Scenario-based benefits/risks 
________________________________________
D: Protecting Data
Must know:
•	Threats (malware, hacking, social engineering) 
•	Protection (encryption, MFA, firewalls) 
•	Impacts of breaches 
Exam focus:
•	“Explain impacts of a cyber attack” (6 marks) 
•	“Describe protection methods” 
________________________________________
E: Impact of IT Systems
Must know:
•	Online services (banking, retail, education) 
•	Targeted marketing 
•	Data collection (primary vs secondary) 
•	Validation vs verification 
Exam focus:
•	“Discuss impacts of IT on organisations” 
•	Data questions 
________________________________________
F: Legal & Ethical
Must know:
•	Data Protection 
•	Computer Misuse 
•	Copyright 
•	Privacy + ethical issues 
Exam focus:
•	6–9 mark discuss/evaluate question
